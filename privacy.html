Privacy policy

This Google sign-in client exists so that its owner can use rclone with their own Google Drive.

What data is used

rclone runs only on the owner's own computer. It reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive when the owner asks it to. Files travel directly between that computer and Google.

What is collected or shared

Nothing. No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with anyone. There is no analytics or tracking.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
